Install on Android

  1. Download the APK using the button above, directly on your Android device.
    If you downloaded it on a computer, transfer the file to your phone first.
  2. Allow the install. When Android prompts you, tap Settings and enable “Allow from this source” for your browser (one time only).
    This appears because the app is distributed directly rather than through the Play Store.
  3. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
    You may see a Play Protect warning for sideloaded apps — choose Install anyway.
